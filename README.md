## 💡 The Problem
E-commerce is broken. **42% of reviews on major platforms are fake**, generated by bots or paid farms. Customers are tricked into buying low-quality products, while honest sellers struggle to compete.

## 🚀 The Solution: TrueView AI
**TrueView AI** is a specialized dashboard that scrapes, analyzes, and scores product reviews in real-time. By leveraging **AMD Instinct™ Compute Credits**, we process thousands of data points to generate a **"Trust Score"** that tells you if a product is legit or a scam.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🕵️ Fake Review Detection:** Identifies bot patterns, repeated phrases, and suspicious timing.
* **📊 The Trust Score:** A simple 0-100 rating for every product.
* **🧠 Sentiment Analysis:** AI reads between the lines—extracting "Pros" and "Cons" from walls of text.
* **📉 Price History:** (Coming Soon) Tracks if a "discount" is real or fake.
* **⚡ AMD Powered:** High-performance analysis using specialized compute credits.
